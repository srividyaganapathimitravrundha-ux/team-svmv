OUR JOURNEY

One Team. One Tradition. One Divine Celebration.

What began as a small friends group of three in 2008, celebrating Lord Ganesha with humility and devotion, has grown year after year into a cherished community celebration.

Through traditional poojas, spiritual and cultural activities, community celebrations and joyful events, Team SVMV honours tradition while creating memories for generations.

Our journey is built on honesty, devotion, unity and the spirit of celebration — growing stronger with every year.
